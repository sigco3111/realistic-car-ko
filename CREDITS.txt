Realistic car: who made what

The tyre model
  Dechode, Godot Advanced Vehicle, MIT License
  https://github.com/Dechode/Godot-Advanced-Vehicle
  The Pacejka curve, combined slip and load sensitivity, ported and changed (commit e055cc3).

The gearbox, suspension rates and assists
  David Shoemaker, Dechode and Baron Wittman, Godot Easy Vehicle Physics, MIT License
  https://github.com/DAShoe1/Godot-Easy-Vehicle-Physics
  Ideas and logic ported from its vehicle.gd and wheel.gd (commit c392257). None of its assets are here.

Righting the car
  Bruno Simon, folio-2025, MIT License
  https://github.com/brunosimon/folio-2025
  What counts as upside down, and hopping to turn over. None of his art, sound or other code is here.

The physics
  Dimforge, Rapier 0.17.3, Apache-2.0
  https://github.com/dimforge/rapier.js

The rendering
  three.js authors, three.js 0.186.1, MIT License
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

The car
  Patch: Vireo rally, CC0 1.0. Its makers and sponsors are made up.

The mannequin
  Patch: the 1.8 m scale figure, CC0 1.0
  Its clips are retargeted from Quaternius' Universal Animation Library 1 and 2, CC0 1.0
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

The sounds
  Real recordings from Freesound (https://freesound.org), all CC0 1.0, cut and levelled by Patch.
  Every recording and its author is listed in audio/CREDITS.txt.

The rally stage's ground, sky and rocks, all CC0 1.0, from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com)
  Road gravel: Forest Ground 04, Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers
  Sealed road: Asphalt 02, Rob Tuytel
  Forest floor: Forest Leaves 02, Rob Tuytel
  Meadow: Leafy Grass, Charlotte Baglioni
  Bare rock: Lichen Rock, Rico Cilliers
  Sky: Kloofendal 38d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest
  Boulders: Rock Moss Set 01, Kless Gyzen

The rally stage's trees
  Daniel Greenheck, ez-tree, MIT License: the pines and aspens are grown with it, and the leaves are its own
  https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree
  Bark: ambientCG, Bark 002 and Bark 003, CC0 1.0
  https://ambientcg.com

The stage's road and land, its grass, poles, boards and gantry
  Patch: made in code, MIT License. The rally and tyre names on the banner are made up.

The yard, its textures, the tyre marks and the dust
  Patch: made in code, CC0 1.0
